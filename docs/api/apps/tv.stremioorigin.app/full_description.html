Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Clean subtitles: Quotes, brackets and music notes are gone; only the marks that matter stay. Works with every subtitle, AI-translated ones too.
  • Your addons' subtitles first: Chosen by themselves from your addons, OpenSubtitles when none has them, and kept in sync quietly in the background.
  • More skips: Skip intro, recap and credits on far more films, series and anime, from more sources.
  • Search you can trust: Results are titles on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and the like. Settings › Playback.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
