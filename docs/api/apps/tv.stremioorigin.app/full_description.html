Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Stremio Origin 2.0 Ultra: A major update: a second player, voice search and the film's own frame rate on Google TV, faster addons everywhere.
  • Every film plays: On Google TV, VLC takes over by itself for any file, picture or sound the TV can't play: from the same point, your language kept.
  • Search by voice: On Google TV, press Search on your remote in Search, or the microphone key, and say a title in English or Arabic.
  • Picture in step, sound everywhere: The screen follows the film's frame rate when Match content frame rate allows it. Dolby and DTS sound from any speakers.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
