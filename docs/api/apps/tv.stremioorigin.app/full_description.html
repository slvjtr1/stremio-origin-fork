Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Anime upscale: Anime only: the TV's Super Resolution and gradation tuned for drawn pictures on the way to 4K, then put back. Settings › Playback.
  • Every title in its own language: Films and series start in the language you choose whenever the file has it, anime in Japanese. Settings › Audio.
  • Smarter search: Your recent searches, an Arabic keyboard, and spelling slips forgiven.
  • Skip, on a timer: The skip button leaves by itself after five seconds, a line showing the time left.
  • Reduce motion: Quicker moves and stiller pages for a slower TV. Settings › Appearance.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
