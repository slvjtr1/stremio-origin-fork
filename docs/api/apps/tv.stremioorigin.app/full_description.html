Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Now on Google TV: Stremio Origin for Google TV and Android TV, on TVs and boxes from 2020 on: the same app, your accounts and settings, updates from inside the app.
  • Made for its remote: The D-pad, OK and Back do everything. Captions, Audio and Info keys go straight to their lists; Menu opens Settings, Search opens Search.
  • Sound and picture as made: On Google TV, Dolby Atmos, DTS and TrueHD go to your receiver as they are, and the screen follows the film's frame rate.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
