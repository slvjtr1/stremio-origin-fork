Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Arabic titles, built in: Titles and descriptions come in Arabic or English with nothing to add. Switch any time in Settings › Language.
  • Links open on your phone: A link from an addon shows a code: scan it and the app's site tells you where it goes, then opens it with one tap.
  • Help in one scan: Settings › About › Share logs opens a page with your code and a report ready to send.
  • Updates that get through: The app checks GitHub, then its site. Rooted TVs can also install and update it from Homebrew Channel.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
