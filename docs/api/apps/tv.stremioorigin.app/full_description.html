Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • A new look: Modern: Black and gold, flat and light, with new line icons and the tabs across the top. Classic stays in Settings, Appearance, Theme.
  • The right episode, clean sources: Fake files, programs and sources of another episode are left out, and a pack plays the episode you opened.
  • Play starts sooner: The best stream is chosen as soon as a good one is there, addons answer side by side, and a real upscale joins Settings.
  • Safer and lighter: A smaller app that keeps to itself on your network, and takes its updates only from this project.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
