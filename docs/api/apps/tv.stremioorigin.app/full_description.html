Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Upscale, your way: Anime and films each with levels C, B and A, A the strongest, and a button in the player to change it as you watch. Settings › Picture and sound.
  • See in the dark: Dark scenes clearer in a bright room, and Brightness for a brighter screen while you watch. Settings › Picture and sound.
  • Clear voice: Speech clearer through the TV's own dialogue enhancer, stereo and Atmos as they are. Even volume too.
  • Accounts: Keep several Stremio accounts on this TV and switch in one press. Settings › Accounts.
  • Only the words: Sound descriptions like [coughs] and (gunshots) hidden, and subtitles lined up sooner and more smoothly.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
